# Compulsory visual design handoff (iteration 007)

## Why this exists

After six iterations, the user still sees an HTML-like slide. V5 and V6 were composed with PptxGenJS coordinates; changing typography and axis selection did not change the underlying CSS/UI grammar. The previous art-direction-first instructions were never operational.

**No direct construction of production slide designs using T/line/band/box helpers before a real slide-image composition is selected.** The PPTX renderer is a reconstruction tool, not an art director.

## New workflow

1. Freeze claim, numerical evidence, source and audience.
2. Research at least two independently published slide or chart precedents with links, visual lesson and reuse restrictions. Study real sample slide images, not only metadata.
3. Produce at least two full-size 16:9 composition images that are substantially different, preferably via SlidePoise image-stage or a visually capable workflow. Do not create both as tiny variations of PptxGenJS output.
4. Critique both full-screen: does it resemble a website/dashboard? Does it show real evidence? Would it fit any subject if the nouns changed?
5. Record an explicit human choice (or rejection), selected design image path + SHA256 and the originating references. No fabricated acceptance.
6. Only then reconstruct editable text, chart objects and annotations. Empirical photos/SEM must have verified source bytes, licence and separate asset provenance.
7. Render and compare the actual PPTX image against the selected composition. Visual acceptance cannot be granted by programmatic shape checks.
